Your money, your data

Nothing leaves
your computer.

MEX doesn't depend on the internet or on anyone's servers. Your numbers live in a file that's yours, protected by 7 layers against loss and snooping.

  1. Power-cut-proof savingWrites to a copy and only swaps it in when everything checks out. Never a half-written file.
  2. Previous version always keptIf something goes wrong, the previous save is right there.
  3. Automatic integrity checkEvery save carries a digital signature. If the file ever gets corrupted, MEX notices and recovers on its own.
  4. Daily backupOne copy a day, kept for 90 days.
  5. Cloud copy, if you wantPoint it to a Google Drive or OneDrive folder and every backup goes there too.
  6. Trash, undo and time machineDeleted something by mistake? Bring it back. Ctrl+Z works too.
  7. Password and PINBackups encrypted with AES-256 and a PIN to open the app.

FAQ

Real questions.

Is the app in English?

Not yet. The MEX app itself is in Brazilian Portuguese and is built for Brazilian small businesses: inside the app, amounts are shown in Brazilian reais (R$), with Pix keys, MEI and WhatsApp reminders.

Does it need the internet?

No. MEX runs 100% on your computer. The internet is only used if you choose to send a reminder on WhatsApp or keep your backup in a cloud folder.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once and the app is yours.

Where is my data stored?

In a file on your computer, in the Documents\MEX folder. Nobody but you has access to it.

What if my computer breaks?

MEX makes a backup every day. If you turn on the copy to a Google Drive or OneDrive folder, your data is in the cloud too. On a new computer, just open MEX and restore the backup.

Is it a good fit for a MEI?

It was built with Brazilian MEIs (individual micro-entrepreneurs), freelancers, service providers and small shops in mind: people who need control without the hassle.

Does MEX issue invoices?

No. MEX handles your finances: money in, money out, orders and reminders. Invoices (nota fiscal) are still issued in your city's or the tax office's system.

Does it work on Mac or on my phone?

For now, only on Windows 10 and 11.

I don't know anything about finance. Will I manage?

It was made for you. No accounting jargon: there's built-in help with questions and answers and a dictionary that translates everything.

What if I don't like it?

You have 7 days to ask for your money back.
